Add durable webhook delivery. `TollwayEvent` now has a stable `id` assigned at emit time, and every delivery carries `Tollway-Event-Id` and `Tollway-Delivery-Id` headers. Pass `outbox` to `createObservabilityHub` to persist one row per deliverable webhook instead of POSTing inline; `createMemoryWebhookOutbox()` and `createSqliteWebhookOutbox({ filename, busyTimeoutMs })` ship. The `WebhookOutbox` interface has `enqueue`, an atomic `claim` that issues a lease token per row, lease-guarded `markDelivered`, `markFailed`, `deadLetter` and `release`, `listDeadLetters`, a one-time `replay`, and `purgeDelivered`. `hub.startDispatcher()` / `hub.stop()` deliver claimed rows with jittered exponential backoff (16 attempts by default), report a lost lease as `WebhookLeaseLostError`, abort open requests before releasing their leases on stop, move exhausted rows to dead letter, and purge delivered rows after `deliveredRetentionMs`. With an outbox, `requestTimeoutMs` must be at least 1 and shorter than `leaseMs`, and `emit` reports a failed write in `failures` instead of rejecting. Subscriptions accept `id` and `secret` (an empty secret is refused); signed attempts are recomputed at send time with a fresh timestamp, and `signWebhookPayload` / `verifyWebhookSignature` are exported. The secret is never stored on a row. Without an `outbox`, `emit` keeps its inline behaviour.
