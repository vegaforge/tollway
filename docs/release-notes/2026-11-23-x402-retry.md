# x402 retries without paying twice

`createClient` now retries an x402 payment that failed in a way that is safe
to retry, and refuses to retry one that may have landed. This is on by default:
`retry.maxAttempts` defaults to 2 (one retry), is clamped to between 1 and 5,
and 1 turns retries off.

- The service challenged the signed payment again, which is how an expired
  authorization or a used nonce shows up. The client checks the policy again,
  then signs a fresh authorization pinned to the amount, asset and payee it
  already signed. A challenge asking for anything else stops the retry.
- The service is already settling the same payment: a 409 with `Retry-After`.
  The client waits, at most 30 seconds, and resends the identical payment
  header. It never signs a new one, so the gate's idempotency answers with
  the original receipt.
- The payment may have landed: a 5xx after the signed request, a dropped
  connection, a 409 without `Retry-After`, or an in-progress settlement the
  client stopped waiting for. `pay()` rejects with `AmbiguousSettlementError`
  (code `outcome-unknown`), and the caller reconciles before paying again.

Each retry emits a `retry` client event. The policy records spend once, after
the payment settles. Settlers classify failures with `SettlementOutcomeError`;
`SettleRequest` gains `pinned` for the terms a retry must match.

The gate now sends `Retry-After: 1` on its "settlement in progress" 409. That
header is what tells the client the 409 is safe to resend: the gate's other
409, a payment already settled for a different offer, has none. Every adapter
already forwards rejection headers. Machine-readable codes for every gate
rejection remain #161.
